## What SHINE actually sells

- **Ilumira** — non-carrier-added lutetium-177 for targeted radioligand cancer therapy, produced
  at the Cassiopeia facility; European marketing authorization secured; shipped to 19 countries.
- **Chrysalis** — a first-of-a-kind molybdenum-99 production facility intended to establish the
  first domestic U.S. commercial supply, backed by a conditional $263M U.S. Department of Energy loan.
- **FLARE** — radiation-effects testing using 14 MeV fusion neutrons for aerospace, defense and
  advanced energy customers.
- **Phoenix Imaging Center** — neutron imaging services (thermal and fast beams, with CT) for
  aerospace and defense; Phoenix LLC merged with SHINE in 2022.
- **Nuclear fuel scanner** — non-destructive inspection of nuclear fuel rods.
- **REDUCE** — used nuclear fuel recycling, recovering uranium and plutonium from spent fuel;
  partners include Orano, Deep Isolation, Argonne National Laboratory and Case Western.
